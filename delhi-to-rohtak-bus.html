Delhi to Rohtak Bus

Delhi to Rohtak Haryana Roadways Buses

Rohtak is one of the most frequently traveled Haryana Roadways routes out of Delhi, connecting the city with one of Haryana's major district hubs. Buses run through the day, making it easy for daily commuters, students, and travelers to plan a journey without much advance notice.

Route Overview

The Delhi–Rohtak route covers a distance of roughly 70–75 km by road, with a typical journey time of around 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic conditions and the number of stops along the way (via Bahadurgarh).

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways operates a mix of Ordinary, AC Deluxe, and select Volvo services on this corridor, so you can choose based on your budget and comfort preference.

Boarding Points

From the Delhi side, buses are commonly available from major ISBT terminals. On the Rohtak side, the main Rohtak Bus Stand serves as the primary arrival/departure point.
