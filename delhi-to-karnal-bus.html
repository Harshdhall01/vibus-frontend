Delhi to Karnal Haryana Roadways Buses

Karnal, known as the "Rice Bowl of India" and a key stop on the Grand Trunk Road, sees frequent Haryana Roadways bus traffic from Delhi throughout the day.

Route Overview

The Delhi–Karnal route covers approximately 120–125 km, with a typical journey time of around 2.5 hours depending on traffic and stops en route.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs Ordinary, AC Deluxe, and Volvo services on this well-traveled GT Road corridor.

Boarding Points

From Delhi, buses typically depart from major ISBT terminals. On arrival, Karnal Bus Stand serves as the main hub.
