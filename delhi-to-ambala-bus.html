Delhi to Ambala Haryana Roadways Buses

Ambala, a key gateway city between Haryana and Punjab, is well served by Haryana Roadways with buses running throughout the day on this longer GT Road route.

Route Overview

The Delhi–Ambala route covers approximately 200–205 km, with a typical journey time of around 4 hours depending on traffic and stops en route.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs Ordinary, AC Deluxe, and Volvo services on this long-distance corridor, with Volvo being a popular option for comfort on the longer journey.

Boarding Points

From Delhi, buses typically depart from major ISBT terminals. On arrival, Ambala Bus Stand (Ambala City/Cantt) is the main hub.
