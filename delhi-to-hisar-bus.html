Delhi to Hisar Haryana Roadways Buses

Hisar is a major industrial and educational hub in Haryana, and buses on the Delhi–Hisar route run regularly throughout the day to serve commuters, students, and business travelers.

Route Overview

The Delhi–Hisar route covers approximately 160–165 km, with a typical journey time of around 3 to 3.5 hours depending on traffic and the number of stops en route.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs Ordinary, AC Deluxe, and select Volvo services on this corridor.

Boarding Points

From Delhi, buses typically depart from major ISBT terminals. On arrival, Hisar Bus Stand is the primary hub for the city.
