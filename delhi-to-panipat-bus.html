Delhi to Panipat Haryana Roadways Buses

Panipat, a major textile hub and historic city on the Grand Trunk Road, is one of the closer and most frequently served destinations from Delhi via Haryana Roadways.

Route Overview

The Delhi–Panipat route covers approximately 90–95 km, with a typical journey time of around 2 hours depending on traffic and stops en route.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs a high frequency of Ordinary, AC Deluxe, and Volvo buses on this heavily traveled GT Road corridor.

Boarding Points

From Delhi, buses typically depart from major ISBT terminals. On arrival, Panipat Bus Stand serves as the primary hub.
