Delhi to Rewari Haryana Roadways Buses

Rewari, an important railway junction town and industrial center in southern Haryana, is regularly served by Haryana Roadways buses from Delhi.

Route Overview

The Delhi–Rewari route covers approximately 80–85 km, with a typical journey time of around 2 hours depending on traffic and stops en route.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs Ordinary and AC Deluxe buses on this corridor.

Boarding Points

From Delhi, buses typically depart from major ISBT terminals. On arrival, Rewari Bus Stand serves as the primary hub.
