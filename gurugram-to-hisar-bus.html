Gurugram to Hisar Haryana Roadways Buses

Gurugram and Hisar are connected by regular Haryana Roadways bus services, linking the NCR corporate hub with this major western Haryana district.

Route Overview

The Gurugram–Hisar route covers approximately 170–180 km, with a typical journey time of around 3.5 to 4 hours depending on traffic and stops en route.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs Ordinary and AC Deluxe buses on this route.

Boarding Points

From Gurugram, buses depart from Gurugram Bus Stand. On arrival, Hisar Bus Stand serves as the primary hub.
