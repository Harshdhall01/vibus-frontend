Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1,2026

ViBus is a bus search tool. This page explains what data we collect and how it's used.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics to understand how many people visit ViBus and which pages are used, in aggregate. This does not use cookies and does not track you individually across other websites.

Local data (favorites & search history)

ViBus stores your recent searches and favorite routes locally in your browser, using your browser's own storage. This data stays on your device — we don't see it, collect it, or store it on our servers. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

What we don't do

We don't require accounts, don't collect personal information like your name or email (unless you choose to email us to report an issue), don't sell data, and don't share data with third parties beyond the analytics provider mentioned above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as ViBus evolves. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.
