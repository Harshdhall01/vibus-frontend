Chandigarh to Ambala Haryana Roadways Buses

Ambala, the closest major Haryana city to Chandigarh, is connected by very frequent Haryana Roadways bus services, making it one of the busiest short-distance corridors in the network.

Route Overview

The Chandigarh–Ambala route covers approximately 45–50 km, with a typical journey time of around 1 to 1.5 hours depending on traffic.

Bus Types Available

Haryana Roadways runs a high frequency of Ordinary and AC Deluxe buses on this short, high-demand corridor.

Boarding Points

From Chandigarh, buses depart from the ISBT Sector 43. On arrival, Ambala Bus Stand (City/Cantt) serves as the primary hub.
