About & Disclaimer

About ViBus

ViBus is a bus search tool built to make it easier to find Haryana Roadways bus routes, timings, and stops in one place.

This is an independent project

ViBus is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Haryana Roadways, the Haryana State Transport Department, or the Government of Haryana in any way. It is built and maintained independently.

About the data

Bus schedules, routes, and timings shown on ViBus are compiled from publicly available sources. We do our best to keep this information accurate, but bus operators can change routes, timings, and AC/non-AC status without notice, and our data may sometimes lag behind or contain errors.

ViBus is currently in beta. We're actively working on improving the accuracy and completeness of our data over time.

Please double-check before you travel

Always confirm departure times and routes with the official Haryana Roadways bus stand, helpline, or website before making travel plans — especially for time-sensitive journeys.

Found something wrong?

We'd genuinely appreciate a report. Email us at support.vibus@gmail.com with as much detail as you can — the bus ID, the route, and what's wrong.
